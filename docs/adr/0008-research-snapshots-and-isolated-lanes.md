# ADR 0008 Independent research lanes and immutable source snapshots

Date: 25 October 2026. Status: implemented for the requested recorded Researcher stage; live access and later workflow stages remain unapproved.

Context: Scout now supplies source-backed candidates and a durable owner-bound approval. The next authorized slice is public page/news research with SSRF/access protections and attributable extraction. No paid calls, deployment or final model selection is authorized.

Decision: require the actual persisted shortlist decision; use at most three independent competitor lanes through a typed research gateway and shared atomic budget journal. Validate public destinations, the full DNS answer set, pinned connection/peer, each redirect and robots/access policy. Keep external transport disabled and execute versioned synthetic recordings. The native TLS pinning primitive is tested with fake sockets; it is not an activated live client.

Persist immutable raw/canonical source snapshots and exact quote/hash/locator links. Recompute deterministic structured/visible-label extraction before creating product/price/claim observations. Preserve per-source contradictions, nullable unknown SKU/weight/pack and explicit marketing attribution. Do not merge uncertain identities/variants or infer clinical efficacy. No provider/model preference is introduced; the quality benchmark remains a separate gate.

Migration 0004 extends the existing owner/RLS schema. Its downgrade rejects evidence that cannot be represented without invention/loss. Integration's dedicated DEMO database cleanup is explicit and separate from that guard. Research completion persists artifacts/readiness only; full LangGraph leases/checkpoint routing, analysis/review/report and frontend integration stay later work. No later-stage execution or external business permission follows from this ADR.

RESEARCH_SPEC.md defines boundaries and limitations; RESEARCH_STAGE_RESULTS.md and RESEARCH_EVAL_RESULTS.json contain actual evidence. Original requirements and acceptance targets are unchanged.
