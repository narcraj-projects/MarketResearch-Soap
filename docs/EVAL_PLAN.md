## Citation presence versus genuine support

Use two separate metrics:

1. Citation presence: material factual assertions with one or more evidence links / all material factual assertions. This is a wiring diagnostic only.
2. Support coverage: material factual assertions with usable supporting evidence / all material factual assertions. This operationalizes A02. Never report presence alone as the BRD score.

Before adjudication, resolve source ID under the same run/owner; verify access status/date, immutable payload hash, exact excerpt membership/location and relevant SKU/variant/pack/date/currency. A valid hash and matching quote prove provenance, not entailment. Then a trusted human/golden annotation checks whether the excerpt actually supports every atomic factual component without overstating its scope. Record supported/contradicted/insufficient, adjudicator and rationale. Review independence/quality/freshness separately; a source quoting marketing supports attribution, not clinical efficacy. A model judge may assist but never substitutes for deterministic or human gates.

Negative controls: a genuine source linked to unrelated text; correct price for wrong pack; quote that contradicts the assertion; brand marketing presented as established efficacy; stale price called current; forged source ID; unsupported compound assertion alongside one supported clause. Presence can be 100% while support is 0%. Do not classify factual premises as hypotheses merely to improve coverage. Marketing attribution is material factual reporting and is included; pure hypotheses/interpretations are scored separately for factual premise support. Missing notices are excluded only when they assert no product fact. Zero factual denominator reports unavailable, never 100%.

Implemented claim_metrics only aggregates trusted adjudication labels. It does NOT resolve sources, verify snippets, assess freshness, determine entailment or authenticate annotators. Contradicted evidence conservatively prevents that claim counting as supported even with another supported link. AgentClaim rejects attempts to self-assign supported/human labels; trusted EvidenceLink ingestion still needs service authorization. V18–V21/V25 validate these distinctions, not actual research quality.
