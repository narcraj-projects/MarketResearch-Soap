## Proposed operating decisions and source resolutions

Queue/body/request/pool bounds are engineering safety defaults introduced for review, not exact BRD metrics or claims about capacity. PRD rate/SSRF/privacy requirements require enforcement even while no paid tools are enabled. Empty research cannot stand in for a failed tool. Cancellation, approval and deadline fences remain authoritative while waiting for capacity. Provider-neutral quality selection is unchanged; no final model/provider is selected, and cheap replay performance does not establish research quality.

Keep all approval-waiting runs in the admission denominator: excluding them would let an approval refill a supposedly bounded queue. Four active worker leases per owner do not imply four simultaneous sets of three held database connections; the engine-wide lane bound protects the shared pool. Owner RLS means this code deliberately does not use a privileged query to count or schedule every tenant. A global scheduler/capacity grant requires a separately reviewed design.

The earlier B13 implementation fingerprint omitted ingress/Auth/queue/lease/storage boundaries. B14 extends that fingerprint; comparison with r2 must report version drift. A new immutable **proposed** r3 baseline requires actual full-suite execution and an independent comparison, retaining r2 and the expected dataset bytes. Baseline review status is not automatically promoted.

## Retention and recovery decisions still needed before hosting

Business retention durations, deletion authority, legal holds, backup retention, RPO/RTO and log/trace storage destinations are required inputs for a hosting/operational policy. They are not supplied as numeric targets in the source documents, so this stage does not invent them or purge anything.

Proposed reviewable approach: owner-requested deletion with explicit authorization; dry-run inventory/counts before mutation; legal-hold and active/pending-approval exclusion; one transactional dependency-ordered purge covering sources/documents/claims/links/products/prices, reports/reviews/evals, checkpoints/writes/tool journals, usage/events/idempotency/jobs/runs/briefs; minimal non-content deletion receipt with a separately chosen lifetime. Backups, sanitized trace copies and Auth identities need independently verified expiry/deletion. No new runtime blanket DELETE privilege. Recovery must restore both application state and approval/checkpoint integrity on a disposable instance and compare hashes. Until that policy and restore evidence are reviewed, retention/deletion/backups remain open release risks.

## Performance interpretation

The benchmark executes real Postgres/RLS, leases, heartbeat, LangGraph checkpoints, three competitor lanes, independent rule analysis/review, durable approvals and save. Recorded web dispatches receive ten milliseconds of artificial delay. DEMO owner approvals are supplied through the normal durable gates with zero human think-time. Each one/two/four-worker condition uses four fresh runs on one local machine, one owner and a shared bounded pool. Timings are observations, not targets; the reported nearest-rank p95 is just the maximum of four samples. No model/provider/network/production capacity, sustained soak, fairness or million-user scalability inference is justified.
